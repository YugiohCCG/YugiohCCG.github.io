# Mendiwake strategy and balance notes

## Identity

Mendiwake is a midrange Fusion strategy about spirits travelling between two
sacred mountains. `Anboto—Cave of Red Storms` develops monsters from the GY;
`Txindoki—Cave of White Mist` finds interaction and recycles spent resources.
The Fields send themselves away and activate the other mountain, triggering the
monsters whenever a face-up card leaves a Field Zone.

The deck is deliberately archetype-locked rather than Type- or Attribute-locked,
because the mythology calls for a mixed group of Spellcaster, Dragon, Plant,
Rock, Aqua, Beast and Beast-Warrior monsters.

## Starter map

- `Mari` is a clean one-card starter that activates Anboto from the Deck.
- `Basajaun` is a one-card starter. It activates Anboto; Journey can use the
  missing named Fusion Material from the Deck after Anboto changes Fields.
- `Basandere` discards itself to find Anboto and later returns when the Field
  changes.
- `Anboto` itself is a one-card starter by sending and reviving Mari.
- Practical opening count at maximum copies: 12 starters before generic search
  cards, with different exposure to Ash Blossom, Ghost Ogre and Droll.

## Core one-card line: Mari

1. Normal Summon Mari; use Mari to activate Anboto from the Deck.
2. Anboto sends Sugaar from the Deck to the GY.
3. Anboto sends itself to the GY to revive Sugaar, then activates Txindoki.
4. Txindoki adds Journey.
5. Journey Fusion Summons Mari and Sugaar—Storm Between Mountains using Mari
   and Sugaar. Sugaar can destroy a card if this line is used going second.
6. The Fusion activates or retains a Field Spell and represents one omni-negate.
   When that negate changes the Field on the opponent's turn, Mari can banish
   itself from the GY to recover a spent Mendiwake Spell/Trap.

Result: one 3200 ATK omni-negate, a live Field engine, graveyard follow-up and no
generic Extra Deck access after Journey resolves.

## Core one-card line: Anboto

1. Activate Anboto and send Mari to the GY.
2. Send Anboto to the GY to revive Mari and activate Txindoki.
3. Mari adds Sugaar; Txindoki adds Journey.
4. Special Summon Sugaar because a Field Spell is face-up.
5. Fusion Summon the boss with Journey.

## Secondary lines

- Basajaun can start without a Field. After Anboto changes to Txindoki, Journey
  may send Mari from the Deck as the missing material. This leaves Basajaun on
  the field as follow-up but applies the Mendiwake Extra Deck lock.
- Basandere plus any extender produces an additional body when the first Field
  changes. It is intentionally banished when it leaves after reviving.
- Tartalo returns a Field Spell to hand to trigger the archetype, then bounces an
  opposing face-up card. It is strongest going second and is a one-of.
- Lamia re-Sets used interaction and filters a poor hand whenever the mountain
  changes. It does not generate an unconditional extra card.
- Jentil is a recurring high-Level payoff. Its negate requires another
  Mendiwake card as real expenditure and does not destroy or banish the target.
- Gaueko answers an activated monster by flipping it face-down. This does not
  negate the already activated effect, preserving counterplay.

## Interaction package

- The Fusion boss: one omni-negate that consumes and replaces the current Field.
- Oath of the Threshold: a searchable Counter Trap with the same Field cost.
- Bell Across the Valley: targeted, non-destruction removal that advances the
  Field cycle.
- The Night Belongs to Gaueko: repeatable Book of Moon-style control.
- Jentil: targeted monster negation paid for with another archetypal card.

A normal one-card opening is intended to end on the Fusion's single negate.
Reaching two or three interruptions should require an additional extender or
access to Eguzkilore/Oath, not happen automatically from every starter.

The 28-card archetypal core can be reduced by trimming the one-of payoffs and
secondary interaction. That leaves room for approximately 12–17 non-engine
cards depending on the desired build.

## Choke points and counterplay

- Negating Anboto's GY setup prevents its clean revive target.
- Removing Anboto before its ignition effect resolves stops the Field transition
  because it must successfully send itself to the GY.
- Banishment or graveyard suppression cuts off Anboto, Mari, Basandere, Journey
  and the Fusion's recovery line.
- Removing the active Field turns off several extenders and makes the boss and
  Oath unable to pay their intended cost.
- Journey's Extra Deck lock prevents the engine from becoming generic material
  for unrelated end boards.
- The boss is vulnerable to non-targeting removal and only floats when an
  opponent's card makes it leave the field.

## Balance levers after testing

If the deck is too strong, first change Txindoki's activation search to only
Mendiwake Traps, or make the Fusion negate destroy instead of banish. If it is
too weak, let Journey's GY effect be used during the turn it was sent there, or
let Dolmen preserve the effects of the first monster it Summons. Avoid removing
the Extra Deck locks: those are what permit strong one-card consistency without
turning Mendiwake into a generic engine.
